How tier distribution affects commissions:
BoW tiers primarily affect consumer visibility (toggle %), not commission rates directly. Commission rates are determined by Wolt's internal tier (Tier 1/2/3/ENT/MM). However, BoW indirectly affects commission mix if: (1) BoW GoV uplift grows the share of lower-commission ENT venues, or (2) multi-tier logic pushes more SMB venues into Tier 3 (highest commission rate).

To calculate commission uplift from tier mix shift:
Pull pre-BoW vs post-BoW shares of Tier 1/2/3/ENT/MM by venue from Looker venue_country_reporting explore, then multiply share changes by their respective commission rates.

How to Complete This Analysis

To fill in the missing columns (GoV, Wolt Tier, Commission Rate), follow these steps:

Step 1 — Get venue-level BoW data from Looker

  1. Open Dashboard 107073 → Venue Overview tab
  2. Filter by your target country/market
  3. Click the tile's ⋮ menu → Download → All Results as CSV
  4. The CSV will include: Venue ID, Venue Name, City, BoW Tier, each metric score

Step 2 — Get commission rates from venue_country_reporting

  1. Open Looker → venue_country_reporting explore
  2. Select dimensions: Venue ID, Venue Name, Wolt Tier, Commission Rate (avg)
  3. Filter: date = last 2 months; country = your target
  4. Download as CSV

Step 3 — Get Pre/Post GoV from purchases explore

  1. Open Looker → f_purchases explore
  2. Pre-BoW period: Filter order_date = Feb 4 – May 3, 2026 (90d before launch)
  3. Post-BoW period: Filter order_date = May 4 – May 17, 2026 (post-launch MTD)
  4. Group by: Venue ID, sum GoV subtotal
  5. Join the two exports by Venue ID → compute % change

Step 4 — Tier Mix Shift Calculation

Formula:
Commission uplift from tier mix = Σ (Post_share_tier_i − Pre_share_tier_i) × Rate_tier_i

Where Rate_tier_i is the commission rate for that Wolt internal tier.
The share change attributable to BoW = share change in months immediately after BoW launch vs a control group (non-BoW cities).
